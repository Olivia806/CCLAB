[ Bibi the Balloon Fish ]

Scientific Name:

Balloon fish

Discovered by / at:

Olivia, March 6 2026

Family:

The balloon fishes usually together under the sea. They have different branches from red to purple. Apparently, Bibi belongs to the purple branch. Scientists have claimed that balloon fishes are not real balloons, but they have certain similar properties to the balloon. For example, their eat bubbles [oxygen] to make their body grows and their skin is similar to balloon, which is very vulnerable.

Habitat:

Mariana trench.

Appearance:

Purple, balloon-like fish. Something different from her family is that Bibi has fins with different colors. Also, Bibi has shining dots on her body and scientists infer this may due to her leaving from the habitat.

Key Characteristics:

  • Body grows slowly as time goes.
  • Eats bubbles.
  • Vunlerable skin.
  • Don't like lights.

What we know so far about Bibi? After conducting series of scientific research, we can now assume that Bibi lives in the very deep sea, namely, the Mariana trench. As the light is almost invisible under the sea, Bibi had very bad eyesight and is very afraid of lights. Every time when scientist trying to interact with her with torch, she falls into panic and swim away. Due to the bad eyesight, Bibi can't find bubbles by herself. She will grow really slow if she don't eat food. By tracking Bibi in Mariana trench, scientist found the trash human discharge has formed dangerous trash under the sea, and has become a threat to balloon fish. Let's help Bibi grow up and survive the dangerous trash!
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature We use torch to interact with Bibi. When the bubble approaches, press the mouse to open the blowing system to make the bubble float to Bibi. Under the sea, trash is nearly invisible and thats why many creatures can be hurt by them. Use the torchlight to discover the trash and prevent Bibi from approaching them!
